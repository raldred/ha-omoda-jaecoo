You can now enter your car's usable battery capacity in the integration options to get an estimated remaining energy reading in kWh.

- Adds **Estimated battery energy remaining**, calculated from battery percentage and your configured usable capacity.
- Capacity is optional and set separately for each vehicle. Leave it blank if you don't want the estimate.
- Missing, stale or unreliable battery data shows as unknown rather than a misleading energy value.

Use usable capacity, not gross/advertised capacity. This is an estimate, not a value reported by the car, and it doesn't adjust for temperature or degradation. No extra API requests are made.

After updating through HACS and restarting HA, open **Settings → Devices & services → Omoda / Jaecoo → Configure** to set the capacity. Existing settings and entities are unchanged unless you enable the new estimate.

Requires Home Assistant 2026.7.1 or newer. [Setup and limitations](https://github.com/raldred/ha-omoda-jaecoo#estimated-battery-energy-remaining).
