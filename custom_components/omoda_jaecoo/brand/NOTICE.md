# Manufacturer artwork — not MIT-licensed

The icon and logo PNGs in this directory are adapted from two replacement brand images supplied by the user: a 400 × 400 OJ monogram with OMODA / JAECOO lettering, and a 1080 × 1080 image of the OMODA / JAECOO wordmark. They are not extracted from a mobile application.

The icon preserves the complete square composition. The logo trims only the wordmark image's surrounding blank background, retaining clear space around the lettering. Both are proportionally resized with Lanczos filtering to 256px and 512px maximum dimensions. White background is converted to transparency; light-theme glyphs are black and dark-theme glyphs are white, using identical alpha masks. No lettering or brand design is invented. Exported PNGs contain no source metadata.
